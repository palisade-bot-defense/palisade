# Community code of conduct

We want a rigorous, welcoming defensive-security community. Be respectful, critique ideas with evidence, disclose conflicts, credit prior work and make room for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, publication of private traffic, unauthorized testing and weaponization of project discussions are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect people or systems.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact once published. Security vulnerabilities belong in GitHub private vulnerability reporting, not in conduct reports or public issues.
